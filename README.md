# Adecco India HR Analytics Dashboard

## Project Overview

This project focuses on analyzing employee attrition and workforce trends at Adecco India using Excel-based HR Analytics techniques. The objective is to identify the key factors influencing employee turnover and provide actionable insights to improve employee retention, engagement, and overall organizational performance.

The analysis was performed using employee demographic, compensation, performance, satisfaction, and tenure data. Various Excel tools such as Pivot Tables, charts, formulas, correlation analysis, and dashboards were used to uncover meaningful business insights.

---

## Business Problem

Adecco India has been experiencing a high employee attrition rate, particularly among junior-level employees. Frequent employee turnover increases recruitment costs, impacts productivity, and affects organizational stability.

The goal of this analysis is to:

* Measure employee attrition rates
* Identify departments with higher turnover
* Analyze factors contributing to attrition
* Evaluate employee satisfaction and engagement
* Provide recommendations to improve retention

## Key Insights

* Employee attrition remains a significant organizational challenge.
* Certain departments exhibit higher turnover rates than others.
* Job satisfaction and employee engagement influence retention.
* Compensation and career growth opportunities impact employee decisions.
* Overtime and work-life balance play an important role in employee retention.
* Training and development initiatives contribute positively to employee engagement.

---

## Business Recommendations

* Strengthen employee engagement programs.
* Improve career growth and promotion opportunities.
* Review compensation and reward structures.
* Enhance work-life balance initiatives.
* Increase training and development opportunities.
* Monitor attrition trends regularly using HR Analytics dashboards.

This project demonstrates how HR Analytics can be used to transform employee data into actionable insights that support strategic decision-making, improve employee retention, and enhance organizational performance.
