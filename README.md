# Adecco HR Analytics Dashboard

An interactive **Excel HR Analytics Dashboard** developed to analyze employee attrition, job satisfaction, and key workforce trends.

## Project Objective

The project focuses on identifying factors associated with employee attrition and providing actionable insights to support employee retention strategies.

## Key Analysis

* Overall Attrition Rate
* Department-wise Attrition
* Gender & Marital Status Analysis
* Job Involvement & Attrition
* Training & Attrition
* Stock Options & Attrition
* Job Satisfaction by Job Role
* Work-Life Balance & Performance
* Employee Tenure Analysis
* Business Travel Analysis

## Dashboard Features

* Interactive Excel Dashboard
* Dynamic KPI Cards
* PivotTables & PivotCharts
* Connected Slicers
* Attrition & Satisfaction Analysis
* Correlation Analysis
* Structured Pivot Backend
* 20 HR analytical questions answered

## Tools Used

**Microsoft Excel | PivotTables | PivotCharts | Excel Formulas | Slicers | Correlation Analysis**

## Key KPIs

* **Total Employees:** 1,470
* **Employees Left:** 237
* **Overall Attrition Rate:** 16.12%
* **Average Tenure:** 7.01 Years
* **Average Job Satisfaction:** 2.73
